2. How did the running times compare for the various dot product implementations?

These were the results from the test run:

dot:          0.62 ns/elt
dot_c:        0.62 ns/elt
dot_struct:   0.76 ns/elt
dot_struct_c: 0.74 ns/elt

The normal dot and dot_c had the same running time
at 0.62 ns/elt, so they were the fastest.

The struct versions were a little slower. dot_struct
took 0.76 ns/elt and dot_struct_c took 0.74 ns/elt.

Overall, the normal array versions were faster than
the struct versions. The C and assembly versions
were pretty close in speed for me.
